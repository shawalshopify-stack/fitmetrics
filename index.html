Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI an accurate health measure? ▾
BMI is a useful screening tool but doesn't directly measure body fat. It doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or fat distribution. Use it alongside other health metrics and consult a healthcare professional for full context.
How is TDEE calculated? ▾
TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) multiplies your BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) by an activity multiplier. We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, widely considered the most accurate for the general population.
Are all the calculators completely free? ▾
Yes — 100% free, forever. No account, no subscription, no hidden fees. FitMetrics is supported by non-intrusive advertising that keeps the lights on.
How accurate is the Body Fat calculator? ▾
The US Navy Method is accurate to within 3–4% for most people. For clinical-grade precision, DEXA scanning is the gold standard but requires a professional appointment.
Do you store my personal data? ▾
No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We never transmit, collect, or store any health data you enter. Your privacy is guaranteed.
