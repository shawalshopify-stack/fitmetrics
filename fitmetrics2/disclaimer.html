Calculator Limitations

Our calculators have inherent limitations:

  • BMI does not account for muscle mass, bone density, or fat distribution. Athletes may have elevated BMI despite low body fat.
  • Calorie calculators use population-average formulas. Actual metabolic rates vary by up to 20% between individuals.
  • Body fat formulas have a margin of error of 3–5% compared to clinical DEXA scanning.
  • Ideal weight formulas are guidelines based on statistical averages, not individual health profiles.
  • Water intake estimates may not account for individual medical conditions, medications, or extreme athletic demands.
