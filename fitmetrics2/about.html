Our Mission

FitMetrics was built with a simple belief: everyone deserves access to accurate, science-backed health tools — for free, forever. Too many health apps are locked behind subscriptions, cluttered with dark patterns, or just plain ugly. We built something better.

Every calculator on FitMetrics uses formulas from peer-reviewed research and WHO guidelines. Your data never leaves your device. No account, no tracking, no nonsense.

Our Values

What guides every decision we make

🔬
Science First
Every formula is sourced from peer-reviewed research. We cite our sources and update when the science evolves.
🔒
Privacy by Design
All calculations happen locally in your browser. We never collect, store, or sell any personal health data.
♿

Free forever. Designed to work beautifully on any device. No barriers between you and your health data.
✨
Beautiful Design
Health tools shouldn't be ugly. We believe great design makes the experience more enjoyable and the results more meaningful.
⚡
Instant Results
No loading screens, no waiting. Your results appear in real time the moment you interact with a calculator.
🌍
Global Standard
Metric and imperial units on every tool. Serving users worldwide with culturally appropriate measurements.
