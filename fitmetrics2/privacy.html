Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

FitMetrics.io ("we", "us") operates fitmetrics.io. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect your privacy.

1. Health Data You Enter

We do not collect any health data you enter into our calculators. All calculations — height, weight, age, measurements — are processed entirely within your browser using JavaScript. This data never reaches our servers.

2. Usage Data

We may collect anonymised usage data such as page views, browser type, device type, and referring website. This data is aggregate and cannot be used to identify individual users.

3. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense). You can opt out of personalised ads at Google Ad Settings. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Google AdSense

We display ads through Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy. EU/EEA users are protected by GDPR; AdSense will request consent before serving personalised ads.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. All data is anonymised. You can opt out via the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We have no control over their content or privacy practices and are not responsible for their policies.

7. Children's Privacy

FitMetrics is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct, delete, and restrict processing of any personal data we hold. Contact us at privacy@fitmetrics.io to exercise these rights.

9. Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be announced on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact
