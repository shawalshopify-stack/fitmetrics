What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a value derived from your height and weight, used as a screening tool to categorise individuals into underweight, normal weight, overweight, and obese ranges. The formula is: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²).

Underweight
BMI below 18.5
Normal Weight
BMI 18.5 – 24.9
Overweight
BMI 25.0 – 29.9
Obese
BMI 30.0 and above

⚠️ BMI is a screening tool only. It doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or fat distribution. Always consult a healthcare professional for a full health assessment.
